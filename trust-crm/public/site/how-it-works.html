How it works

From your first message to following up on your applications.

  1. 1. Register

    Send your details and agree to their processing. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 2. We may contact you

    A member of our team reviews your details and may contact you to talk about your situation.

  3. 3. Prepare your profile

    Together we prepare an accurate CV and the information employers ask for.

  4. 4. Apply to real offers

    We apply to offers that fit your profile. You approve each application before it is sent.

  5. 5. Follow the replies

    We track employer replies and tell you the next step. The final decision always belongs to the employer and the authorities.
