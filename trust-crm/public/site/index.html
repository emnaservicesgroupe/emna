What VITA does

Search

We look for job opportunities that fit your profile. Offers are entered by our staff along with a note of where each offer came from. Always verify the details yourself, and never pay anyone for a job.

Prepare

We help you prepare an accurate CV and application. Nothing is sent in your name without your approval.

Follow up

We keep track of replies and next steps, so you always know where each application stands.

What we do not promise

Hiring decisions belong to employers, and work permits and visas are decided by government authorities. VITA cannot promise a job, sponsorship, a permit or a visa.
