Destinations

Where we focus our work.

Canada (first focus)

Canada is the first destination we work on. Requirements, opportunities and processing times are set by Canadian employers and authorities and change often. We share only what we have checked, and we cannot promise any result.

  • Requirements differ by occupation and by province.
  • Ability in English and/or French is usually important.
  • Work permits are decided by the Canadian authorities, not by VITA.
  • Be careful with anyone who guarantees a job or a visa.

Other destinations

We will add other destinations only when we have offers we can stand behind. You can ask us about a country through the contact page.
