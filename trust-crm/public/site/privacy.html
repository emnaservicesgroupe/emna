Privacy

A plain summary of how this website handles your details.

What we collect

Only what you type in our forms: name, email, phone, country, profession, company and your message. We also record the time you agreed and which consent wording you saw.

Why we collect it

To contact you about job opportunities or your inquiry, and to manage your registration with our team.

Who can see it

Authorised VITA staff, through the company's internal system. Your details are never shown on this website.

Technical data

We do not store your IP address. To limit abuse we keep a one-way scrambled version of it. This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies; it only remembers your language choice in your browser.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, through the contact page.

Important notice

This service helps you search and apply for job opportunities. It does not guarantee employment, employer sponsorship, a work permit or a visa.
