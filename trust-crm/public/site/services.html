Services

What we can do for candidates and for employers.

For candidates

Profile review

We review your experience, skills and languages with you and note what is missing.

Job search support

We look for opportunities that match your profile and tell you about the ones that fit.

Application preparation

We help you prepare a truthful CV and application. You approve every application before it is sent.

Follow-up

We record employer replies and keep you informed about the next step.

For employers

Employers can tell us which profiles they are looking for. We introduce candidates whose profiles match, and the employer decides.

Conditions and any fees are explained to you individually before you commit to anything.

Important notice

This service helps you search and apply for job opportunities. It does not guarantee employment, employer sponsorship, a work permit or a visa.

VITA

VITA helps candidates search and apply for job opportunities abroad.
