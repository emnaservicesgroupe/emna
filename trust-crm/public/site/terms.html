Terms of use

Please read these terms before using this website.

What this website is

An information and contact service that helps candidates search and apply for job opportunities, and helps employers present their needs.

No guarantee

Using this website or registering does not guarantee employment, employer sponsorship, a work permit or a visa. Those decisions belong to employers and government authorities.

Offers

Offers are published by VITA staff from the information given by the employer. They can change or close at any time. Check the details with the employer before you act.

Your information

Give true and complete information. False information can end your registration.

Be careful

Do not pay money to anyone who promises you a job, a permit or a visa.

Changes

We may update this website and these terms. The version on this page is the current one.
